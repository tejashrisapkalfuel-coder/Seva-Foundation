Official Trust Deed Mandate

Our Vision & Mission

Comprehensive strategy driving Swarajya Seva Foundation.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive, empowered and sustainable society where every individual—especially rural students, farmers, women, children, senior citizens and vulnerable communities—has access to education, healthcare, livelihood opportunities and the resources needed to live with dignity and achieve a better future.

Our Mission

Swarajya Seva Foundation is committed to creating meaningful and sustainable social impact through integrated initiatives in education, health and agriculture:

  • Empower rural & underserved students through scholarships and career guidance.
  • Improve healthcare access through health camps and medical assistance.
  • Empower farmers through sustainable agriculture, soil health & skill workshops.
