Who We Are

About Swarajya Seva Foundation

Grounded in communities. Driven by purpose. Transparent in action.

Education is our primary sector, reflecting the most detailed objectives in our Trust Deed (Sections 3.1a–h). We focus on:

  • Rural student career counselling & mentorship
  • Scholarship awareness & financial assistance
  • Schools, colleges & digital learning centres
  • Books, uniforms, stationery & learning resources
  • Computer literacy & digital education
  • Skill development & personality development
  • Seminars, career guidance camps & webinars
  • Support for children with special needs

Covered under Section 3.2 of Trust Deed — we bring healthcare access to those who need it most:

  • Rural health camps & vaccination drives
  • Blood donation drives
  • Medicine & medical kit distribution
  • Maternal & child healthcare
  • Mental health awareness & counseling
  • Disability & senior citizen care

Our differentiating pillar under Section 3.3 — empowering farmers through sustainable practices:

  • Organic farming & soil testing
  • Farmer skill-development workshops
  • Counseling for financial challenges
  • Ecological & forest conservation
  • Water conservation & irrigation guidance
  • Connecting farmers to market & tech

Our Vision

To build an inclusive, empowered and sustainable society where every individual — especially rural students, farmers, women, children, senior citizens and vulnerable communities — has access to education, healthcare, livelihood opportunities and the resources needed to live with dignity.

Our Mission

Swarajya Seva Foundation is committed to creating meaningful social impact through integrated initiatives — empowering rural students with scholarships & career guidance, improving healthcare access, and equipping farmers with modern, sustainable tools and knowledge.

Our Programs

What We Do — Our Key Programs

Hands-on initiatives designed to build careers, confidence, and communities.

Career Guidance

One-on-one career counseling, mentorship sessions, and guidance camps for rural students to discover their strengths and chart the right path — from stream selection to professional careers.

  • Personalized career roadmaps
  • College & stream counseling
  • Expert mentorship network
Get Guidance

Scholarship Program

Merit and need-based scholarships connecting deserving students directly with compassionate sponsors. Transparent process, verified applications, and direct sponsor-student connection.

  • Need & merit-based selection
  • Direct sponsor-student connection
  • Full document verification

Entrepreneurship Workshops

Practical workshops that ignite the entrepreneurial spirit in rural youth — from business ideation and skill development to pitching and self-employment opportunities in both traditional and emerging sectors.

  • Business ideation & planning
  • Agri-entrepreneurship & start-ups
  • Self-employment skill building
